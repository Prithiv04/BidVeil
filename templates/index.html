Private data handling

  1. Documents are uploaded to BIDVEIL for this evaluation.
  2. Readable text is extracted in request-scoped processing.
  3. Bid text is sent to NEAR AI Cloud for analysis.
  4. Model TEE verification is shown only after the official SDK validates a provider_tee signature. A gateway signature is never treated as model TEE proof.
  5. Temporary server-side files are removed after the request, including failures.
  6. BIDVEIL does not intentionally persist uploaded files or extracted bid text.

BIDVEIL cannot guarantee physical memory erasure or control NEAR AI Cloud’s retention policies. Do not send information unless the service’s terms are acceptable to your organization.
