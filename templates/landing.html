BIDVEIL records the evaluation lifecycle, document validation, text extraction, and temporary cleanup. Record and uploaded document hashes are checked against their stored values. A hash check does not authenticate source claims or origin. Provider retention policies are outside BIDVEIL's control.

About BIDVEIL

BIDVEIL is a confidential procurement intelligence platform designed for organizations that require structured, transparent, and accountable tender evaluation processes.

Technology

Built with Flask, Python, and vanilla JavaScript. No unnecessary dependencies or complex frameworks.

Privacy

Documents are processed ephemerally. Original files are never stored. Temporary directories are cleaned after each request.

Transparency

Evaluation criteria, weights, and scoring algorithms are deterministic and visible. AI never selects a winner.
